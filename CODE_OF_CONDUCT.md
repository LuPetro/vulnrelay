# Community guidelines

VulnRelay welcomes constructive contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and help others reproduce and understand problems.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, credential disclosure and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Keep discussions focused on the project. Do not publish private asset inventories or vulnerability details that should be reported privately.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, request changes in behavior, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate, consistent and explainable. People involved in a conflict should not make the final moderation decision alone when another impartial maintainer is available.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an available private contact channel. For software vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md. Do not put personal details or confidential evidence in a public issue. No response-time guarantee is implied for this volunteer project.
